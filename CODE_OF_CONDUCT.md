# Code of Conduct

Speech Buddy is built for people working on their communication. Our community should feel just as safe.

## Our standards

- Be kind, patient and respectful
- Welcome people of every background and ability
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully
- Never mock speech differences, accents or disabilities

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language
- Sharing anyone's personal or health information without consent
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers by opening a private issue or contacting a team member. Maintainers may remove content or contributors that break this code.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
